# Repository visibility and content rights

This repository is publicly readable so Agents and humans can inspect the catalog, contracts, examples, and review documentation. Public GitHub visibility does not by itself grant permission to copy, modify, redistribute, sell, or execute every prompt solution.

The effective content policy is the `rights` field in each CLI-generated `solution.json`, together with the `license` and `permissions` fields in each template contract. At present, many official solutions use `rights: internal` and `license: internal`; those values remain restrictive even though the repository is public.

External references and canaries keep their own terms. For example, `video-shotcraft` source is Apache-2.0, Remotion has a separate license, and bundled media may have per-file attribution or commercial-use limits. Referencing an external repository does not copy its license onto Yeisme templates or onto user-provided assets.

Public synthetic fixtures and examples are provided for inspection and validation. They do not transfer confirmation state, provider permission, credentials, product-asset rights, publishing authority, or canonical acceptance into a real user session.

Tool and binary distribution terms are maintained by their owning repositories. Template content rights, software licenses, model/provider terms, and user asset rights must be evaluated separately.
