# manga-panel-v1

- Readiness: exploratory.
- Common contract: 30–90 seconds and 8–16 key shots or panels; valid typed direction takes precedence.
- Core job: make pose, silhouette, action phase, screen direction, depth layers, and subject relationships readable in a single frame.
- Action strategy: split complex action into anticipation, contact, and result; do not force an unreadable continuous process into one image.
- Panel rhythm: establishing, conflict, and result panels must carry different information; do not fill page rhythm with repeated expressions.
- Composition: prioritize clear silhouette, action line, gaze direction, and negative space; text safe areas may not cover faces, hands, or key props.
- Continuity: lock SubjectVersion, wardrobe layers, footwear shape, prop hand, facing direction, position, and action carryover.
- Image handoff: every panel provides an independently generatable subject, action phase, scene anchor, composition, lighting, and negative constraints.
- Review: panel readability, subject continuity, screen direction, action phase, text safe area, and generatability of the image instruction.
