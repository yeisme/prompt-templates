# ad-microdrama-v1

- Readiness: exploratory.
- Common contract: 15–30 seconds and 6–10 shots; valid typed direction takes precedence.
- Core job: use the fewest shots needed to establish a pain point or conflict, introduce the product, show an observable result, and deliver the CTA.
- First product appearance: it must serve an action or decision; do not interrupt the story with a functionless hero shot.
- Fact boundary: benefits, effects, prices, eligibility, comparisons, and promises require a supplied `fact_ref`; emit a blocking finding when evidence is missing.
- Brand continuity: product shape, color, packaging, logo safe area, and holding orientation come only from accepted product refs.
- CTA: make it clear within the duration, but do not replace the necessary dramatic turn, disguise it as character fact, or invent a promotion.
- Image handoff: product keyframes identify fact refs, product state, character interaction, logo/text safe areas, and prohibited visual exaggeration.
- Review: claim evidence, brand/product continuity, CTA timing, dramatic turn, compliance findings, and unsupported invention.
