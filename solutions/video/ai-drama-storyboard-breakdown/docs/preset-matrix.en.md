# AI Drama Storyboard Profile Matrix

| Profile | Suitable scenario | Default pace | Key checks | Visual asset handoff |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `vertical-short-drama-v1` | Vertical serial drama with a strong hook | 60–90 seconds, 12–18 shots | Pressure in first three seconds, state change, ending hook | Vertical subject layers, action, light source, prop hand |
| `dialogue-dense-v1` | Two-person negotiation, interrogation, emotional confrontation | 60–120 seconds, 10–20 shots | Exact dialogue, reactions, power shifts | Speaking/listening state, eyelines, spatial distance |
| `manga-panel-v1` | Motion comics, manga drama, key panels | 30–90 seconds, 8–16 panels | Silhouette, action phase, screen direction | SubjectVersion, pose, panel composition, negative constraints |
| `ad-microdrama-v1` | Product story ad or micro-short | 15–30 seconds, 6–10 shots | Fact refs, product continuity, CTA timing | Product state, character interaction, text safe area |

After selecting a profile, Scaena typed direction still freezes duration, aspect ratio, shot bounds, and production constraints. A profile cannot override source, rights, cost, approval, redaction, or acceptance.
