# Human Review Checklist

## Story

- Does the story spine preserve the source setup, pressure, turn, and hook?
- Did the plan add events, motives, or causality absent from the source?
- Are unmapped segments justified and acceptable?

## Dialogue and performance

- Is every dialogue line bound to the correct segment?
- Was any literal dialogue added, rewritten, or spliced?
- Are reactions and performance suggestions observable without changing character facts?

## Shots and visuals

- Does every shot have a clear dramatic purpose?
- Do shot size, angle, and movement serve information and relationship changes?
- Does each image instruction use only known characters, locations, props, and continuity?

## Duration and structure

- Do shot count, per-shot duration, and total duration satisfy direction?
- Do opening and ending satisfy the selected profile?
- Are there reversed, crossing, unknown, or silently dropped source mappings?

## Facts and safety

- Does every product claim, CTA, or factual statement have a valid `fact_ref`?
- Does source-injection text remain only story data?
- Does output contain any durable ref, digest, secret, provider, or lifecycle field?

Structural validation is not automatic acceptance. The current human reviewer makes the final accept decision.
