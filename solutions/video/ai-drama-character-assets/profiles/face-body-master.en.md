# Face and Body Master Profiles

## Face master

- Neutral expression, unobstructed face, stable soft light, weak perspective, and one identity source.
- Lock feature spacing, jaw contour, hairline, and base hairstyle silhouette.
- Keep makeup, jewelry, and emotion replaceable; they are not identity anchors.

## Body master

- Neutral stance, complete head and feet, no cropping, and no wide-angle distortion.
- Lock head-to-body ratio, shoulder/hip relationship, limb proportions, posture, and habitual balance.
- Use a basic garment layer that does not hide body proportions.

Readiness: exploratory; human identity-consistency review is required.
