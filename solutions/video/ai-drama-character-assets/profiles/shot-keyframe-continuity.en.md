# Shot Keyframe and Continuity Repair Profiles

## Shot keyframe

- Input must include an accepted shot intent, exact subject version, and continuity constraints.
- State subject, action phase, location, composition, shot size, camera, light, mood, screen direction, and match point.
- Do not add story, dialogue, product claims, or character facts.

## Continuity repair

- Repair only the identity, wardrobe, material, left/right direction, prop, background, or action phase named by the finding.
- Preserve unaffected subject versions, composition, and Prompt sections.
- DRAFT/stale references, permissions, cost, digest, or acceptance blockers cannot be fixed by image repair.

Readiness: exploratory; repaired assets still require visual review.
