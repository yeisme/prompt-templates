# Prop and Scene Asset Profiles

## Prop sheet

- Lock size, material, construction, functional state, wear, and grip relationship.
- Front/back/side or open/closed states share the same construction facts.
- Unknown writing, brands, mechanisms, and functions remain unknown.

## Scene concept

- Lock entrances, exits, performance zones, key light sources, reusable background anchors, and required prop locations.
- Day/night or mood variants must not change the architectural plan.
- Do not add story events, crowds, or unsupported brand elements.

Readiness: exploratory.
