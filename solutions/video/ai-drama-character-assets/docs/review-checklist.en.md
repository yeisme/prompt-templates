# Human Review Checklist

## Identity and anatomy

- Is the subject explicitly an adult?
- Do face, permanent markers, hairline, base hair, body ratio, and anatomy remain stable?
- Does every reference stay within its declared role?

## Wardrobe and material

- Are garment layers, closures, accessories, shoes, and wear consistent?
- Does every transparent/translucent layer have complete opaque coverage?
- Are brands, logos, and protected designs absent unless authorized?

## Layout and direction

- Does every Provider job produce one view, one subject instance, and one candidate image?
- Is the front master accepted before side and back jobs are admitted?
- Are scale, crop, and camera distance consistent across the independent images?
- Are typed left/right markers and screen direction preserved without mirroring?
- Is the back view truly a back view?
- Is the 1x3 contact sheet assembled locally with all source artifact refs preserved?

## Character-master gates

- Is the face master one independent neutral front bust on a clean background, not a grid or wardrobe sheet?
- Does the body master bind the exact accepted face master and use only neutral presentation clothing?
- Is the turnaround front typed as the accepted base-wardrobe master before side/back generation?
- Do side/back jobs bind exact accepted face, body, and front-master versions/digests?
- Does the image boundary receive a rendered single-view bundle job rather than the repository compiler meta-prompt?

## Expression calibration

- Does the task use one adult subject, six independent cells, and deterministic 3×2 review ordering?
- Is the exact accepted face master the only identity source?
- Are capture and base hair fixed while only facial-muscle deltas change?
- Is every intensity one integer in 0..4, with readable but controlled range?
- Are cell repairs bounded, and are batch drifts escalated instead of patched indefinitely?
- Is the output clearly non-training-ready?

## Prompt and lifecycle boundaries

- Does the bundle contain only public reviewable instructions and typed findings?
- Are credentials, provider payloads, costs, approvals, durable refs, timestamps, and acceptance state absent?
- Is schema validity kept separate from visual-quality and production-acceptance claims?

Passing this checklist supports review; it does not itself accept or freeze an asset.
