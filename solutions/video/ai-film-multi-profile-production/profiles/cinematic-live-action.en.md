# Profile: Cinematic Live Action

`profile_id`: `cinematic_live_action`. Maturity: `exploratory`; readiness: `candidate`; rights: use only the characters, locations, props, audio, and delivery rights that the consuming owner has already projected and confirmed as available; never presume likeness, music, trademark, or performer-voice rights.

The shared state semantics stay: source projection → active bindings → semantic proposal → owner candidate/review; this profile creates no canonical ref, digest, acceptance, or provider run.

Risk priorities: identity stability, observable performance, hand and prop contact, center-of-gravity/cloth/weight physics, light-source logic, lip sync, and spatial sound. Minimum evidence: safe binding summaries for face/wardrobe/location/prop, the SceneGEO, the shot duration window, and a visible evidence summary; multi-character work additionally needs the axis and screen direction.

Suggested duration: generate single segments in a reviewable 4–8 second window by default; split complex action into shorter coverage, with final duration owned by the Scaena/Sonora owners. Readiness may only be suggested as prototype when identity, GEO, first frame, audio-visual responsibility, and a low-risk alternative all have evidence; never claim production-ready or final.

Profile-specific QC: a head turn must not rebuild identity; wardrobe and wound states stay continuous; physics before and after contact agree; non-speaker reactions leak no information; a stable two-character coverage can replace a high-risk hero shot.
