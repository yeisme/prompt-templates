# Profile: Stylized Animation

`profile_id`: `stylized_animation`. Maturity: `exploratory`; readiness: `candidate`; rights: use only the style, character, asset, and audio rights the owner projected explicitly; never treat similarity to a reference style, character, or IP as authorization.

The shared state semantics stay: source projection → active bindings → semantic proposal → owner candidate/review; accept no assets, produce no canonical revision/digest, start no render.

Risk priorities: shape language, turnaround, deformation limits, color/material consistency, motion rhythm, and multi-view identity readability. Minimum evidence: the style rule projection, binding summaries for face/body/wardrobe/turnaround, allowed and forbidden deformations, the scene palette/light rules, and the duration window.

Suggested duration: keep a single action beat within 2–6 seconds; split exaggerated deformation, transformations, or multi-character tracking into setup/contact/result. Final shot length, frame rate, and delivery specs belong to the owner. Readiness may only be suggested as prototype/candidate and never upgraded on the strength of one pretty frame.

Profile-specific QC: expression sheets stay face-level assets; wardrobe never rewrites identity anchors; deformation never crosses stated limits; front/back/side views remain the same character; color and material states stay continuous across shots.
