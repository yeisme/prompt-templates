# Profile: Brand Film

`profile_id`: `brand_film`. Maturity: `exploratory`; readiness: `candidate`; rights: product facts, claims, CTA, trademarks, people, locations, music, and delivery territories must all come from the owner's safely verified projection; anything not provided is unknown and must not be invented or softened past legal/brand review.

The shared state semantics stay: source projection → active bindings → semantic proposal → owner candidate/review; this profile publishes nothing, accepts no claim, and produces no final advertisement or canonical release.

Risk priorities: product truth, evidence-backed claims, logo/text geometry, CTA, rights, and duration/territory limits. Minimum evidence: product fact ref summaries, allowed claim text and banned words, logo/text placement rules, the rights/timing/territory projection, and the audio/music rights policy.

Suggested duration: validate a single product or CTA visual beat in 2–6 seconds for clarity; full advertisement length and the legal/brand delivery gate belong to the owner. Readiness can only be candidate; missing product facts or rights evidence must block.

Profile-specific QC: never invent performance, price, certification, or comparative claims; the CTA stays readable within the given duration; logo/text never distorts or occludes; product state and package version trace back to a safe binding; a missing fact ref returns `MISSING_PRODUCT_FACT_REF`.
