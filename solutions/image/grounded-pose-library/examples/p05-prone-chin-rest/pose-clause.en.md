Grounded pose, prone with the chin cradled in both hands: The body lies prone, both knees bent so the lower legs kick up, the feet close together or crossed. Both elbows rest on the ground, forearms rising vertically, hands cradling the cheeks or chin. The chest lifts slightly off the ground with a gentle extension through the upper back; the head stays raised. Weight spreads across the abdomen, pelvis and both elbows; the outline stays relaxed, the legs forming an upward arc.
