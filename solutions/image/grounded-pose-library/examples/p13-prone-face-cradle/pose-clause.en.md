Grounded pose, prone with the face cradled in both hands: The stomach lies against the ground, both knees bent so the lower legs swing up, the ankles or feet crossed. Both elbows rest on the ground in front of the chest, hands cradling the face from both sides. The chest lifts slightly, the head centered. The body stretches mainly horizontal, the raised legs forming a second point of interest; the whole reads as nearly left-right symmetric.
