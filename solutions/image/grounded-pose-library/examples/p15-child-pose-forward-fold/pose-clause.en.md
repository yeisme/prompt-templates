Grounded pose, kneeling forward fold (child's pose): Both knees bend and the seat settles toward the heels, the legs folded beneath the body. Both arms stretch completely forward, palms flat on the ground. The chest and stomach fold down toward the thighs, the head lowering close to the ground. A low, stable weight center; the silhouette descends steadily from the highest point at the hips toward the hands.
