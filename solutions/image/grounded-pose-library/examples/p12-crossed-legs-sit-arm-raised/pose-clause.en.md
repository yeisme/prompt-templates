Grounded pose, seated with crossed legs and one arm raised: The legs cross and fold in front of the body, one leg passing in front of the other. One hand props on the ground behind and to the side while the other arm lifts, bends, and brings the hand behind the head. The chest rises with a slight side-bend through the waist; the head faces forward. The arms sit at two different heights for a clear asymmetry; the overall line feels stretched and open.
