Grounded pose, side-lying with the head propped on one hand: Both legs bend and stack along one side of the body, the knees pointing forward or slightly up. One hand props the head with the elbow on the ground; the other forearm rests on the ground in front of the chest for support. The torso lies on its side but the chest opens slightly toward the front of the frame; the head tilts toward the supporting hand. Weight rests on the side hip, the side leg and the elbow; the figure forms a classic reclining triangle.
