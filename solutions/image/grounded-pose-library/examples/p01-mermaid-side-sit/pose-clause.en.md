Grounded pose, mermaid side-sit with rear support: Both legs bend and gather to one side of the body, one thigh angling forward while the other leg folds behind, the calves lying close together. Both arms reach behind the body, palms pressing the ground, elbows almost straight. The chest lifts upward and the waist arches slightly back; the head rises and turns toward the upper side. Weight rests mainly on the hips and both hands, the body tracing an S-line from lifted chest through arched waist to legs spreading sideways.
