Grounded pose, kneeling turn-back seen from a rear three-quarter angle: Both knees rest on the ground slightly apart, the lower legs folded back and the seat clearly raised off the heels. Both arms hang at the sides and slightly behind the body, hands near the ground or close to the thighs. The upper body rotates backward from the pelvis, the shoulder line visibly twisted; the head turns toward the rear side. Weight rests on both knees and lower legs; the counter-twist between hips, waist and shoulders is the point of the pose.
