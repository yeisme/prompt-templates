Grounded pose, frontal kneel with the knees wide open: Both knees open far to each side, the lower legs folded back, the seat settling between the legs. Both arms reach straight down, hands placed together on the ground directly in front of the body. The upper body sits almost upright with the shoulders drawn slightly forward; the head faces straight ahead. A highly symmetric composition: the knees form a wide triangular base and the arms a single central vertical.
