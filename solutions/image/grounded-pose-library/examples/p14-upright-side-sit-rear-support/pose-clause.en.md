Grounded pose, upright side-sit with rear support: Both legs bend at once and gather to one side of the body, thigh and calf layered. The arms are placed behind the body, one hand clearly bearing weight on the ground. The chest lifts, the pelvis sits settled, the upper body leaning back a little; the head frontal or slightly turned. The hips are the main anchor with the rear hand steadying the balance; the carriage stays fairly upright and open.
