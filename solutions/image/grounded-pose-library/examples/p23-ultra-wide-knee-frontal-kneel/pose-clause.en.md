Grounded pose, frontal kneel with the knees ultra-wide: Both knees open to the sides at their widest, the lower legs folded back or inward, the pelvis sitting low. Both arms hang vertically, the hands stacked or side by side on the ground directly in front. The upper body upright with a slight forward lean, the shoulders centered, the head frontal. Like the frontal wide kneel but wider and lower, the horizontal outline even more emphatic.
