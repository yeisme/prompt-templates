Grounded pose, side-sit leaning back on one hand: Both legs bend and gather toward the same side, one leg in front, the other folded behind. One hand is placed on the ground behind the body, the other resting close to the body or a leg. The upper body leans back yet keeps the chest open, the shoulders carrying a slight rotation; the head upright. Weight concentrates on the hips and the rear supporting hand; the legs spread sideways while the torso forms a backward diagonal.
