Grounded pose, side-sit propped on one hand behind: One leg bends in front of the body while the other folds away to the side, the two legs loosely overlapping. One hand rests near a leg or knee, the other placed behind the body to prop on the ground. The chest stays open and the upper body leans slightly back toward the supporting hand; the head stays level. The hips and the rear supporting hand carry most of the weight; an easy, casual seated posture.
