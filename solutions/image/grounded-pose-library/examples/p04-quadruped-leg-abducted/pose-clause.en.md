Grounded pose, quadruped kneel with one leg abducted: One knee supports on the ground while the other hip opens outward, that knee bent and lifted away to the side. Both hands press the ground, arms nearly vertical to the floor. The torso lies close to horizontal with a slight arch in the back; the head turns to face the front of the frame. A classic three- or four-point support with the seat held high; the side-lifted knee gives the pose a clear asymmetry.
