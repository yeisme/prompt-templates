Grounded pose, back-turned side-sit with rear support and twist: Both legs fold toward the same side, one leg passing in front while the other tucks near the hip. At least one hand props on the ground behind the body, the other arm staying close behind the torso. The chest lifts as the upper body rotates backward, the shoulder line and the pelvis pointing different ways; the head follows the turn. The lumbar twist is the essence; a clear spiral line runs from the hips through the shoulders.
