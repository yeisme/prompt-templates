Grounded pose, kneeling on the forearms with the hips high: Both knees and lower legs support on the ground, the knees bent, the seat clearly lifted. Both forearms, not the palms, rest on the ground, the arms bent. The chest hangs below the hips, the back sloping downward; the head low and turned to one side. The forearms and knees carry the weight; front-low and rear-high, the body pressed even lower than a hands-forward kneel.
