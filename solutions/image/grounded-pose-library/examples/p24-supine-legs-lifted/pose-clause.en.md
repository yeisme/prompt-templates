Grounded pose, supine with the knees lifted off the ground: Both legs leave the ground, the hips and knees deeply bent, the shins folding back and upward, the feet drawn close. One arm extends along the ground to one side, the other bends close to the head, shoulder or upper body. The upper body turns slightly sideways, the head and shoulders just off the ground or tilted to one side. The lifted legs become the main visual focus; upper and lower body point different ways with a light twist.
