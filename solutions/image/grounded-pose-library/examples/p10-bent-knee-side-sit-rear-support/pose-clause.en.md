Grounded pose, side-sit with one knee bent up and rear support: One leg bends upright with the sole near the front of the body; the other folds away to the side. One hand rests on the raised knee, the other placed behind the body to prop on the ground. The upper body leans slightly back and tilts toward the supporting hand; the head turns just a little sideways. Hips, one foot and the rear hand form a three-point support; the outline carries a strong diagonal.
