# Editorial Half-Face Cover Template

A reusable style layer for magazine-cover-grade luxury beauty portraits built from a reference person. The core is not restating the reference photo but: an extreme close-up of exactly half the face split along the vertical centerline, the subject placed at the right edge with hair volume kept in frame, a wide clean left negative space for typography, and premium editorial finishing with visible skin and hair detail. Only the persona, the slogan, and the signature are swapped per image; every style paragraph stays fixed. Bind all variables before rendering; the rendered result is the final prompt body without further rewriting.

{{format_clause}}Using the attached reference person, generate a surreal, advertising-grade, high-end artistic portrait. Do not copy or simply crop the reference image; instead, while preserving the subject's characteristic face, hairstyle and hair color, fringe, gaze, expression, makeup, and clothing impression, reconstruct an entirely new high-end visual work with fresh composition.

Identity impressions to preserve from the reference: {{persona}}.

The composition must satisfy every requirement below, strictly:
- The subject occupies the right side of the frame, in a near-frontal extreme close-up of the face.
- The face must be cut precisely in half along the vertical centerline, the split path running: center of the forehead → glabella → bridge of the nose → nose tip → center of the lips → center of the chin.
- Only half a face may appear in frame, and nothing more: one eye, one eyebrow, half a nose, half a mouth, half a chin.
- The other half of the face must remain entirely outside the frame.
- Two-thirds face, diagonal crops, profile views, full face, and any crop that misses the centerline are forbidden.
- While the face is enlarged, the crown of the head and the volume of the hairstyle must stay inside the frame.
- The left side of the frame keeps a broad, elegant, clean negative space reserved for text.

Subject rendering, with realism as the highest priority:
- Preserve the reference subject's facial proportions and every bound identity impression above; keep the quiet, poised, high-end poise of the reference rather than any posed studio look.
- The skin must not be over-smoothed or plastic: clearly render pores, fine skin grain, natural shadows, vellus hair, lip texture, and individually distinguishable hair strands.
- The overall image carries translucency, dimensionality, soft luster, and surreal detail — the finish of a fashion magazine cover, a premium cosmetics campaign, a photo-collection key visual, or a brand poster.

Background:
- Choose the background tone automatically to flatter the subject, guided by the subject's hair color, skin tone, iris color, clothing, lighting, and overall atmosphere.
- The background style is elegant, minimal, soft, pure, and airy; use high-end light palettes such as cream white, pearl white, champagne gold, misty gold, very pale warm gray, or soft misty beige.
- The background must stay uncluttered and must never compete with the subject.

Campaign line:
- In the lower left, add one short campaign line exclusive to the subject, written from the subject's visual impression and fit for advertising, a photo collection, or brand visuals; not a poem, not vague boilerplate.
- The line is short, memorable, refined, and unique, in Japanese or English — whichever better suits the mood of the piece.
- Render this exact line: {{slogan}}

Signature:
- Near the campaign line in the lower left, add a smaller, discreet signature that must display exactly, character for character, including every bracket and symbol: {{signature}}
- Do not alter any character, punctuation mark, bracket, or symbol of the signature. Its font, color, and ornamentation harmonize with the whole image, and its size stays smaller than the campaign line.

Typography:
- The campaign line sits in the lower left, set in an elegant script, calligraphic, high-end serif, or refined Japanese typeface suited to the mood of the piece.
- The text color coordinates with the background automatically — light gold, warm gray-gold, pale champagne gold, or soft beige gold are preferred.
- A few refined decorative elements are allowed — thin lines, small blossoms, leaves, twigs, arcs — but readability is mandatory and over-decoration is forbidden.

Final requirements:
- Subject on the right, near-frontal, exactly half the face, extreme close-up, crown and hairstyle inside the frame, broad negative space on the left, background elegant and minimal and tuned to the subject, campaign line and signature in the lower left, signature text exact.
- Unrelated marks, watermarks, and stray text are forbidden; the finished piece reads as a premium campaign portrait, a brand-ad key visual, a fashion photo-collection cover.
