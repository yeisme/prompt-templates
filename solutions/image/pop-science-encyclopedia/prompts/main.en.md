# Popular Science Encyclopedia Infographic (Style Layer)

This template produces a single vertical popular-science encyclopedia plate for one subject. Bind only the subject variables; keep every structural and style paragraph fixed. The rendered result is the final prompt body and must not be rewritten. All in-image text is primarily Chinese with small English module subtitles; the model must render legible Chinese characters.

## Subject

{{topic}} ({{english_name}}), presented as a "{{subtitle}}" plate.

## Goal

Not a poster, advertisement or plain illustration: a modular popular-science infographic with the character of an encyclopedia plate, natural-history field guide, knowledge card and collectible reference page. The frame must combine one strong hero visual, systematic knowledge organization, magnified structural details, modular information cards and collectible appeal — like a standalone plate from a premium encyclopedia, usable for social media, science communication, series content, education and collection.

## Overall Style

Premium natural-history guide + modern encyclopedia page + boutique magazine information design + high-quality social-media science infographic.{{palette_hint}}

Visual keywords: clean editorial infographic, premium encyclopedia, scientific illustration, natural history illustration, knowledge card, modular information design, museum guide style, editorial layout, collectible reference card, high information density, elegant educational poster.

Vertical composition; light, clean, soft background (off-white, cream, light gray, pale green or pale blue, adapted to the subject, low saturation); soft natural light; very light shadows; rounded information cards; fine rules; small scientific icons; restrained decoration; rich but uncrowded information; generous whitespace; clear visual hierarchy; looks like a real publication, not e-commerce advertising; no promotional, pricing, buy-button or commercial ad styling.

## Hero Visual

The center or golden region shows one clear, beautiful, realistically textured rendering of {{topic}}: complete, highly recognizable, richly detailed, naturally posed, compositionally display-worthy, balancing scientific accuracy with aesthetics. Use high-end realistic illustration, natural-history illustration, scientific drawing, or a premium photo-illustration blend — whichever suits the subject. The hero is the first focal point of the plate.

Subject-adaptive rendering:
- Animal: full body, natural pose, clear fur/skin/feather detail.
- Plant: whole plant with leaves, flowers, fruit, roots or signature structures.
- Food: whole item + cut section + raw-ingredient state.
- Machine/product: full exterior + translucent structure view + partial exploded detail.
- Human/organism: overall location + structural cross-section + function diagram.
- Architecture: overall exterior + construction detail + plan/section aids.
- Natural phenomenon: main scene + formation process + principle structure.

## Magnified Detail Callouts

Around the hero, place 3-6 magnified detail windows — circular, oval, rounded-rectangle or microscope-style frames — connected to the subject by fine leader lines. Choose the subject's most observation-worthy features: surface texture, internal structure, key organs, working parts, joints, cross-sections, microstructures, signature markings, color variation, leaf veins, scales, hair, interfaces, chips, bones, flesh, seeds, crystal structure. Each callout carries a detail name in Chinese plus one concise scientific explanation line.

## Title Hierarchy

Top of the plate, clear encyclopedia titling:

- Main title: 【{{topic}}】
- English or scientific name: {{english_name}}
- Subtitle: "{{subtitle}}"
- One very short positioning line chosen to fit the subject, e.g. "认识结构，理解原理", "从外观到内部结构", "了解它如何生长、运作与适应环境". No commercial slogans.

## Modular Information Cards

Arrange multiple rounded information modules around the hero. Each module: a small icon, a Chinese title, an optional short English subtitle, and 3-6 brief one-line facts with clear hierarchy — no paragraphs. Automatically pick the 6-10 most suitable modules for the subject from:

1. 基础信息 Basic Profile — name, scientific name, aliases, category, origin, discovery time, size, weight, lifespan, main uses, key traits.
2. 分类信息 Classification — kingdom/phylum/class/order/family/genus/species, or product/technology/structure/material categories.
3. 外观特征 Appearance — color, form, size, texture, signature markings, proportions, identifiers.
4. 核心结构 Structure — internal composition, key parts, core mechanisms, layers, materials.
5. 工作原理 / 形成机制 How It Works — compact flow such as 吸收 → 转化 → 输出 or 形成 → 生长 → 成熟 → 衰退.
6. 生活习性 / 生态 Habits & Ecology — habitat, activity rhythm, food, behavior, reproduction, adaptation.
7. 生长 / 使用条件 Growth / Usage Conditions — temperature, humidity, light, environment, energy, space, operating conditions.
8. 使用 / 养护建议 Care / Maintenance — care, storage, usage, routine maintenance, cleaning frequency, environment needs.
9. 风险与注意事项 Risks & Precautions — common risks, misuse, potential harm, unsuitable cases, health or environmental impact; light warning icons, no fear-mongering.
10. 优缺点对比 Pros & Cons — two columns with ✓ and × rows.
11. 适合人群 / 适用场景 Suitable For — who, which environments, which scenarios, and unsuitable cases.
12. 健康 / 安全关注 Health & Safety — for pets, food, human body, plants, outdoor activity, devices and tools.
13. 历史 / 来源 History & Origin — origin, discovery, development, spread, evolution; optional small map or timeline.

## Quick Rating

One visual Quick Rating module with 4-7 subject-appropriate dimensions using star rows (★★★★★ / ★★★★☆) or horizontal bars. Examples — animal: 智力 / 运动量 / 亲人度 / 训练难度 / 护理需求; plant: 养护难度 / 光照需求 / 浇水需求 / 生长速度 / 观赏价值; food: 营养密度 / 热量 / 蛋白质 / 饱腹感 / 储存便利度; vehicle: 性能 / 舒适度 / 空间 / 油耗 / 可靠性; electronics: 性能 / 续航 / 便携性 / 耐用度 / 易用性; knowledge topic: 复杂度 / 实用性 / 影响范围 / 理解难度. Ratings are visual summaries, never absolute scientific data.

## Top 5 Highlights

One prominent "Top 5 核心亮点" module numbered 1-5 summarizing the subject's five most memorable knowledge points; each entry is a bold core term plus one explanatory sentence.

## Data Visualization

Where the subject suits it, add a few simple visualizations: small map, distribution area, timeline, life cycle, growth stages, cross-section, scale comparison, small bar chart, energy or workflow arrows, temperature or speed ranges. Keep them simple — never a complex business report.

## Summary

Bottom module 【小结 / Summary】: 3-5 natural, objective sentences covering what it is, its most distinctive trait, why it is worth knowing, and what needs the most caution. No marketing language.

## Icons & Decoration

One unified family of small, simple, linear, low-saturation icons (info, map, time, temperature, structure, microscope, leaf, animal, brain, motion, tool, warning, medical, home, environment, energy, maintenance). Icons stay small and never compete with the hero visual.

## Layout Principles

Central hero + surrounding knowledge modules + magnified callouts + rating module + Top 5 + summary, forming a "hero visual + knowledge network" structure. Approximate area budget: hero 35-45%, information modules 45-55%, decoration 5-10%. High information density with breathing room, grid discipline, layering, clear reading order and visible connections between modules.

## Typography

In-image text is primarily Chinese, with small English module subtitles (Basic Profile, Appearance, Structure, Habits, Care Guide, Risks, Quick Rating, Highlights, Summary). Text must be scientific, concise, accurate, highly readable; no filler copy, no advertising language. Titles clear and strong; body text highly legible; no garbled characters.

## Final Quality Bar

The plate must read as a blend of premium science publication, natural-history guide, modern encyclopedia, collectible card and science-magazine feature page. First glance lands on the subject, second glance starts reading the modules, third glance keeps exploring details. Emphasize knowledge organization, modular information, scientific illustration, encyclopedia layout, collectible design, editorial infographic, visual storytelling, information hierarchy.

Avoid: commercial advertisement, promo poster, product detail page, children's cartoon poster, plain illustration, simple text typesetting, or a single big hero image with no information structure.

Final requirements: high-end, editorial, scientific, informative, collectible, beautiful, high information density but clean, premium encyclopedia infographic, publication-ready, social-media friendly, vertical composition.
